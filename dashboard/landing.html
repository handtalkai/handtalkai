About us

Bridging communication gaps with AI

HandTalk AI is a capstone project designed to bridge communication gaps by translating Sign Language into readable text and audible speech using AI-powered gesture recognition.

The system uses a webcam to perform real-time hand gesture recognition, converts recognized gestures into text, generates natural speech output, and keeps a history of past sessions so users can track and revisit their conversations.

Features

Everything you need to communicate

Real-Time Gesture Recognition

Recognize Sign Language hand gestures instantly using AI-powered computer vision powered by MediaPipe and TensorFlow.

Text Conversion

Convert recognized gestures into readable words and complete sentences in real time.

Text-to-Speech

Speak translated sentences naturally using the built-in Text-to-Speech system.

Gesture Dictionary

Browse supported Sign Language gestures with visual references and organized categories.
