Best Practices

Recognition Tips

Follow these tips for the best gesture recognition accuracy

Ensure Good Lighting
Use a well-lit environment so the camera can clearly see the outline of your hand. Avoid strong backlighting or shadows that hide finger positions.
Stay Within the Frame
Keep your entire hand — from wrist to fingertips — visible inside the camera frame throughout the gesture for reliable tracking.
Perform Gestures Steadily
Hold each gesture clearly and steadily for at least half a second. Quick or shaky movements can be misread as a different letter.
Avoid Cluttered Backgrounds
Use a plain or simple background so the model can focus on your hand instead of competing shapes and colors behind it.

Quick Help

Common issues and how to fix them — click any question to expand.

  • Make sure your browser has camera permission granted. Look for the camera icon in the address bar.
  • Close any other apps that might be using the camera (Zoom, Teams, Camera app, etc.).
  • Try refreshing the page, then click Start Feed again.
  • If using Windows, check Privacy & Security → Camera in Settings to confirm browser access is allowed.
  • Try a different browser (Chrome or Edge are recommended).
  • Ensure there is enough light on your hand — natural or desk lighting works best.
  • Keep your full hand clearly inside the camera frame.
  • Hold each gesture steady for at least half a second before moving on.
  • Avoid wearing gloves or accessories that cover your fingers.
  • Try a plain, uncluttered background to help the model focus on your hand.
  • Check the Gesture Dictionary to confirm you are performing the correct hand shape.
  • Check that your system volume is not muted and is turned up.
  • Make sure no browser permission is blocking audio output.
  • Try clicking the Speak button manually in the Translator page.
  • Some browsers restrict auto-play audio — interact with the page first (click anywhere) and try again.
  • If using the SPEAK gesture, hold the thumbs-up gesture for the full 1.5 seconds until the progress bar completes.
A letter is added after the same gesture is held for 20 consecutive frames. To add a letter faster, hold the gesture more steadily without moving between frames. To slow down, briefly relax your hand between gestures.
